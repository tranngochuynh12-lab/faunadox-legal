Privacy Policy for FAUNADOX Control

Last updated: 20 October 2026

FAUNADOX Control is a private desktop tool operated by one independent creator who owns the YouTube channels FAUNADOX, 肩の荷おろし帖, 心の雨宿り帳【心理学・脳科学】 and Twice Told Economics, and the matching Facebook Page and TikTok account. It is used only to upload the owner's own videos to these accounts.

YouTube API Services

FAUNADOX Control uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's Privacy Policy applies: https://policies.google.com/privacy

What we access

With the account owner's permission (OAuth, one sign-in per account), the tool uploads videos, captions and thumbnails to that account, schedules their public time, and reads the account name to confirm which account is connected. It does not access other users' data.

Storage

Access tokens are stored only on the owner's own computer, in a local file that is never shared, published or sent to any third party. No data from YouTube, Facebook or TikTok is sold or shared.

Revoking access and deleting data

Access can be removed at any time: Google at myaccount.google.com/permissions, Facebook in Settings > Business Integrations, TikTok in Settings > Security & permissions > Manage app permissions. Local tokens are then unusable and are deleted from the computer. To ask for deletion of any data held by the tool, email the contact below.
